## ✨ About MyVerse

**MyVerse** is a personal Progressive Web App (PWA) designed to collect, organize and rate your favorite movies and songs in one place.

A private space where your passions become your own universe.

No accounts.  
No tracking.  
No unnecessary data collection.

Everything stays on your device.

---

## 🎬 Features

### Movies
- Add your favorite movies
- Add cover images
- Rate movies with stars ⭐
- Add personal notes
- Save where you watched them (Netflix, Prime Video, etc.)
- Mark favorites ❤️
- View detailed information

### 🎵 Music
- Add your favorite songs
- Save artists and covers
- Rate songs ⭐
- Add personal notes
- Mark favorites ❤️
- View detailed information

---

## 🚀 Progressive Web App

MyVerse works as a PWA:

- 📱 Installable on smartphones
- 💻 Works on desktop
- ⚡ Fast loading
- 🌙 App-like experience
- 🔒 Local storage system

---

## 🔐 Privacy

MyVerse respects your privacy.

- No user accounts
- No personal data collection
- No external database
- All content is stored locally on your device

Your universe stays yours.
